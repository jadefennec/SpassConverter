SPASS Converter lets you migrate your saved passwords out of Samsung Pass and into any standard password manager.

Pick your encrypted .spass export file, enter your Samsung Pass password, and the app decrypts it locally and produces a CSV file you can import into Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass, or any other tool that accepts CSV imports.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No internet connection — ever. All network permissions are explicitly blocked in the manifest.
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs.
• Your passwords never leave your device. The .spass file is decrypted entirely in memory and the CSV is written directly to wherever you choose to save it.
• Sensitive state (password, decrypted data) is cleared automatically when you leave the app.

FEATURES
• Decrypts Samsung Pass .spass export files
• Exports logins, credit cards, and addresses to CSV
• UTF-8 BOM in output for correct display in Excel and Google Sheets
• Tap-to-preview the converted CSV before saving
• Resolves app package names to human-readable service names and URLs
• Material 3 UI, supports dark mode and large screens
• Android 8.0+ (API 26)
