Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

SPASS Converter is an Android app that converts Samsung Pass export files (.spass) to CSV format. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

Data Processing

  • Local processing only: All conversion happens on your device. Your Samsung Pass export file and password are processed entirely on-device.
  • No transmission: No data, files, or passwords are ever sent off your device. The app does not use the internet or any network connection.
  • No collection or storage: We do not collect, store, or share any of your data with third parties. We do not have servers that receive your information. We do not use analytics or tracking.
  • Temporary files: When you tap "preview" to open the CSV in another app, a temporary file is created in the app's private cache. It is deleted when you leave the app or convert another file. Other apps cannot access this cache.
